How it works, and how it has done

A reconstruction of the method RotoBot describes in its posts (“every snap is built from real 2026 play-by-play”), written from public nflverse data. None of RotoBot's code, data or app was used.

  1. Play call. Pass or run from a league model of down, distance, field position, score and clock, shifted by each offense's own pass rate over expected. Fourth downs follow the league's go, kick and punt rates for that spot.
  2. Defensive look. The defense blitzes at its own charted rate. Man and zone coverage is not public for 2026, so the blitz is the scheme the sim knows.
  3. Dropback. Sack, scramble, throw-away or target, combining league rates for that look with the QB's and the defense's.
  4. Outcome. One of the ball carrier's real plays from a similar down and distance, kept or redrawn according to how this QB, line and defense change that kind of result. The play brings its yards, catch and turnover; the clock comes from real snaps in the same game state.
