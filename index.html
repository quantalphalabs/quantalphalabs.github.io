Findings

Five things we tested. None of them beat the market.

Each was pre-registered before it was run, evaluated walk-forward, and judged against a default-deny checklist that returns no edge unless every gate passes.

Method

Why you can believe the number.

Leakage firewall

Every feature carries the timestamp it became knowable, and there is exactly one sanctioned way to join features to games. A feature that could have seen the future is refused, not warned about.

Pre-registration

6 experiments, each with a hypothesis and a predicted direction written down before the run. The configuration is hashed; changing it and re-running raises an error.

Sealed holdout

1,139 games kept unreadable behind a hash-chained ledger. Opening it is logged permanently and mechanically widens every interval afterwards.

Placebo nulls

A thousand models that know nothing are run through the identical pipeline. If noise reproduces our result, our result is noise — and twice it was.

Default deny

The verdict returns no edge unless every blocking gate passes. The vocabulary stops at edge candidate; nothing in the system can print the word profitable.

Walk-forward only

Models are fitted on prior seasons and scored on the next. Fitting on everything and scoring in-sample inflated one result from +3.5% to +8.9% before the harness caught it.
