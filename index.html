Coverage charting is not public, so this is the honest version: each receiver against the opponent’s overall defensive rank. 1 is best.

Offence against defence

League rank, 1 best, from last completed season. Defence is ranked on EPA allowed per play from our own play-by-play, not on yards — ESPN publishes a yards-allowed field but never fills it. A strong passing offence against a bottom-five defence is where volume comes from.

Anytime touchdown

Probability that each eligible player reaches the end zone. A quarterback’s number is his rushing touchdown chance — that is what the market pays, so passing scores are excluded.

These are calibrated probabilities, not edges. On development seasons the model beats the position base rate by 8.6% Brier skill with 0.6% calibration error, which is skill at the task. No anytime-touchdown price has ever been pulled here, so nothing below has met a market. The fair price carries no margin and is our probability written as odds — a gap between it and a real screen is not value.
